Open `3DS EXPERIMENTAL` from the library. This preview uses an embedded emulator engine. It does not use the DS player.

Tap `Open 3DS game` and select your own decrypted cartridge dump in CCI format. The app copies it into private storage. A full cartridge may require several gigabytes of free space. CIA installers and archives are not accepted.

The preview has stacked screens, touch input and game buttons. The Circle Pad area supports gentle movement for sneaking. Save normally inside the game. These saves are separate from the Game Boy and DS saves.

Tap `Omega Ruby guide` for a searchable offline story guide with individual steps and manual completion tracking. The game pauses while you read. The guide has not been checked through a complete playthrough. It does not yet contain every item or encounter table.

This is an experimental integration. Compatibility and speed depend on your device and still need testing. The live party, bag, battle, map and progress readers are not implemented for 3DS. A guide step you check is your own record, not progress read from the game.
